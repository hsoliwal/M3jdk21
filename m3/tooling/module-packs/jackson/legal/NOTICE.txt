The Jackson annotation, core and databind implementations remain the work of
their upstream FasterXML authors and contributors. They are compiled from the
existing, unchanged M3JDK21 source inclusions at version 2.19.2 and retain their
original Apache-2.0 legal texts and notices. This integration does not rename or
relicense those implementations.

Jackson core's existing Maven build shades the existing source inclusion of
Werner Randelshofer's FastDoubleParser 1.0.90. Its MIT license, original notices,
and third-party license texts are preserved independently. The build uses its
existing JDK 11, 17 and 21 overlays; JDK 22-only overlays are outside this JDK 21
package.

SOURCE_LOCK.json pins the exact source/build/legal trees and the existing M3
JMOD assembler. Every resulting JMOD receives the complete selected legal bundle.
Its source-built upstream JAR resources retain their own provenance and notices.

The decoded indexed-I/O M3File adapter derives its occurrence/offset model from
Synexia MIndexFile and retains MIndexFileTokenStore's adaptive storage logic under
their Apache-2.0 grant. Per-file source paths, the exact source commit and Git blob
identities are retained in the Java headers and the M3 Jackson name-mapping record.

The public String API remains java.lang.String. Actual M3-backed String storage
requires a qualified M3JDK21 runtime; passing these packaging contracts on a stock
JDK does not establish that separate VM acceptance gate.
