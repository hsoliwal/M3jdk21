## Source dispositions

Keep the four Java modifications already emitted and semantically qualified:
`M3IndexedArray`, `M3IntAlgorithms`, `CollectionViews` and
`M3ArrayifiedIndexOps`. Preserve the corrected additive CPU probe and all seven
other additive contract/resource fixtures. The original public/protected APIs,
JNI declarations, protected tests and unchanged receiver owners remain sealed.

The new core CPU diagnostic verified identical generated probe classes, all
24 fresh JVMs and all original workload checksums. Its 15-case audit is
`qualification/resources/core-cpu-audit-v1/AUDIT.json`, SHA256
`48d48ab9b57d9ab32fb0abc3f5b013781c8c3f2f859856fb436f68cbebb67382`.
All 12 lonely-array/filtered-view cases allocate less in every pair. The three
sorting cases have equal allocation. No case has three or four CPU ratios over
1.10; individual ratios and higher medians remain mixed. This is not a new
threshold or a blanket CPU pass. The sorted-refresh32 and dense4096 observations
are explicit review cautions. The original elapsed-only signals are retained,
not relabelled as CPU. These Java changes are component-qualified review
candidates with the stated resource evidence, not universal speedup claims.

The independently qualified array-index owner reduces allocation in all six
native-selection cases; some CPU observations are mixed. Its detached rotation
and stable merge preserve the measured ownership and order contracts. No
per-workload CPU claim is generalized to the integrated product.

Keep the incumbent `M3LongBitmapFilteredSnapshot`. Its optional packed-occurrence
candidate has a confirmed CPU veto across the two complete cohorts. Keep the
incumbent native `m3_index_mask_compactor.c` as well. Native v1 and the reviewed
pointer-traversal v2 both passed semantic/ABI/UBSan/real-JNI checks and reduced
instrumented payload transfer, but failed the unchanged complete CPU matrix.
V2 recorded 25 flagged cases, including changed nonempty cases. Controls and
host variability remain reported; they do not waive the frozen admission rule.
No width, density, length or mask threshold is fitted to favorable observations.
