The initial implementation was a deterministic source relocation of the
Android array/index implementation. Two subsequent M3 refinements repair
dense-index relocation defects exposed by legal inverse forcePut sequences:
captured predecessor/successor indexes are rebased before adjacency checks,
and a captured predecessor is rebased after swap-last deletion. These
bounded changes preserve the storage form and ordinary valid behavior;
they intentionally do not reproduce the donor's broken iteration states.
The package and
class name change to com.m3.collections.guava.M3HashBiMap. The implementation
directly implements the public Guava BiMap API and extends JDK AbstractMap;
views extend JDK AbstractSet. There is no Guava collection implementation
superclass or delegated collection owner.

Only these bounded support surfaces are lifted into package-private nested
types: Hashing.smear/smearedHash/closedTableSize and their constants;
CollectPreconditions.checkNonnegative(int)/checkRemove;
ImmutableCollection.Builder.expandedCapacity (renamed Capacity);
the AbstractMapEntry API tuple base; both NullnessCasts methods;
ParametricNullness; Serialization.readCount/writeMap/populateMap(map,
stream,size). Their selected method bodies remain donor text.

Hashing's Ints.MAX_POWER_OF_TWO dependency becomes the identical expression
1 << (Integer.SIZE - 2), verified in the same commit at
android/guava/src/com/google/common/primitives/Ints.java,
blob e5a4d7155d3582833d33c6385927d12ad640384f. Hashing's unneeded
needsResizing method is omitted. Its existing MurmurHash3 public-domain
attribution remains in the source. No other primitive helper is copied.

The public Guava BiMap API, scalar Preconditions/Objects utilities, Guava
platform annotations, Error Prone/J2ObjC annotations and JSpecify nullable
annotations remain compile dependencies. The package-private parametric
nullness annotation is lifted; it does not become a new public API.

Forward and inverse receive own serialVersionUID 1L for strict compilation.
Concrete class identity and donor wire identity are not claimed. Except
for those two contract repairs, this bookkeeping and the mechanical
relocation/support closure, the Android donor's order, representative
selection, inherited Map defaults, mutation timing, callbacks, views and
entry rebinding remain unchanged.

ANDROID IS NOT THE JRE IMPLEMENTATION. The source relocation does not admit
JRE parity. In particular, inherited replaceAll, forEach dispatch and
equal-but-distinct representative selection require actual JRE
discriminators before any separately pinned protocol refinement. The
initial generated receipt binds prototype custody only. That prototype
was rejected by the broader invariant test; its receipt and failures are
preserved. Final scoped execution and the two refinements have separate
provenance and qualification records.

The before-code FOSS_REUSE_DECISION.tsv, candidate-evaluation.tsv and
INVENTORY.json are required inputs and are bound into the relocation
receipt. Actual behavior qualification and source receiving recipes are
separate executed proof steps; this helper does not replace them.
