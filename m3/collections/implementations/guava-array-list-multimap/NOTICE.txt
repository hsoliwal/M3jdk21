Apache-2.0 public interfaces com.google.common.collect.Multimap,
ListMultimap and Multiset with nested Multiset.Entry. Original Guava Authors
copyright and Apache License 2.0 headers remain in every source.
Modified 2026 by Hitesh Soliwal and contributors: compile-only GWT,
ErrorProne and JSpecify annotations removed; default method helpers
reimplemented using the JDK; the Multiset source is reused from Synexia's
canonical Guava Multiset recipe, not independently hand-patched.
Actual Guava jar is retained ONLY as a test-scope differential oracle and
is not a downstream runtime dependency of this isolated artifact.
Other Guava provider modules contain some matching FQCNs; do not co-load
these individual jars or the upstream Guava jar until they share one
source-built ABI and a proven JPMS/classloader closure.
